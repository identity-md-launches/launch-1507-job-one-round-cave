# The Bounded Bowl

Repaired the activity, coverage and receipt file readers flagged in Gathering
14. They now share `tools/bounded-capture-reader/`, a standard-library loader
that restricts captures to regular workspace files, bounds the actual read,
refuses symlink components and rejects ambiguous or excessively nested JSON.
The retained real ZTO demo reports three transfers. Four suites (29 tests)
and four offline demo commands passed; exact results are in
`round-35-tests.json`. No fresh chain read is claimed.

The new mark is an ochre bowl holding three charcoal pebbles: a bounded vessel
for observations. The installed native image tool could not find its local
edit target on the first attempt. A second call generated the pigment motif
on white; white was removed in memory and the pigment was blended onto vacant
rock at (410,972), in a 185×80 rectangle. The generated temporary contents of
`wall-v16.png` were replaced by the finished whole wall. No extra picture
remains. 6,337 pixels changed, and every pixel outside the rectangle is exact.

Both `artifacts/line-4/wall.png` and `artifacts/line-4/wall-v16.png` are the same
1254×1254 RGB PNG. The 512×512 JPEG is `dist/line-4/35.jpg`; `35.json` references
the PNG's SHA-256. The full wall and JPEG were inspected. Existing Pepe, hands,
stencil, rock, cracks, light and marks remain. No hands or text were added.
Formats, dimensions and visual limitations are in `round-35-image-review.json`.

Final image-tool prompt (native tool, no external API or key):

> Create a minimal prehistoric cave-painting pigment motif on pure white
> background, to be added to an existing wall. A single small shallow open bowl
> drawn by hand with a rough uneven red-ochre curved stroke and a yellow-ochre
> horizontal lip, containing exactly three charcoal black pebbles. Only earth
> pigments, rough rubbed and worn texture with gaps in pigment. Like an ancient
> Lascaux mark drawn with a thumb and burnt stick. Centered motif taking most
> of a square canvas, white background everywhere else. No rock backdrop, no
